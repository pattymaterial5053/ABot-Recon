## 🎯 What Is ABot-Recon?

ABot-Recon is a powerful 3D reconstruction tool that turns ordinary video footage into detailed 3D models. Think of it as a digital sculptor that watches video streams and builds accurate 3D representations of the scenes in real time. Whether you're working with drone footage, security camera streams, or any long video recording, ABot-Recon helps you capture the spatial details that other tools miss.

Unlike traditional 3D reconstruction software that struggles with long videos, ABot-Recon uses a clever technique called "local context revisiting." This means it remembers important details from earlier parts of your video while processing new frames, producing smoother and more accurate 3D models even when the video is hours long.

## 🖱️ How to Use ABot-Recon

### 📂 Loading Your Video

1. Click the **"Open Video"** button on the main screen.
2. Browse to your video file (supported formats: MP4, AVI, MOV, MKV).

The video will appear in the preview window, and 3D reconstruction will begin automatically.

### 🎛️ Adjusting Reconstruction Settings

Use the control panel on the right side to fine-tune your results:

- **Quality Slider:** Drag left for faster processing, right for higher detail.
- **Memory Size:** Increase this value if your video has lots of repetitive scenes.

When you're satisfied with the reconstruction:

2. Choose a format (PLY, OBJ, or PCD are recommended).

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### 🤔 Why is my 3D model incomplete?

This usually happens when the video has too much motion blur or the camera moves too quickly. Try lowering the Quality Slider to "Balanced" mode, or use a slower, more stable video.

### ⏳ How long does processing take?

Processing speed depends on your hardware and video length. As a rough guide: a 10-minute 1080p video takes about 5 minutes on recommended hardware. Longer videos scale proportionally.

### 💾 What file formats can I export?

ABot-Recon supports PLY, OBJ, PCD, and XYZ formats. PLY is recommended for most uses as it preserves color information.

### 🔄 Can I pause and resume processing?

Yes! Click the **"Pause"** button to stop processing temporarily. Your progress is saved automatically. Click **"Resume"** to continue from where you left off.

### 🛡️ Is my data safe?

ABot-Recon processes everything locally on your computer. No video data is uploaded to any server. Your footage remains completely private.

## 📚 Tips for Best Results

- **Use stable footage:** Mount your camera on a tripod or gimbal for smoother reconstructions.
- **Avoid extreme lighting changes:** Sudden brightness shifts can confuse the reconstruction algorithm.
- **Overlap your shots:** If recording manually, ensure each frame overlaps with the previous one by at least 70%.
- **Process in chunks:** For very long videos (over 2 hours), consider splitting them into segments and merging the outputs later.

## 🧰 Troubleshooting

### 🚫 Application won't start

1. Ensure you have the latest graphics drivers installed from NVIDIA or AMD.
2. Right-click the ABot-Recon icon and select **"Run as administrator"**.
3. Check that your Windows version is 10 or newer.

### 🎨 Colors look wrong in the 3D model

This can happen with HDR videos. Go to **Settings > Color Correction** and enable **"Standard Dynamic Range"** mode.

### 💾 Not enough memory error

Close other programs while running ABot-Recon. If the problem persists, reduce the Quality Slider to "Fast" mode.
